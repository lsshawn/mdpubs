This agreement is between [Client Name] ("Client") and [Your Business Name] ("Manager"), effective [Start Date]. The Manager will run the Client's social media accounts on a monthly retainer under the terms below.

## 1. Services

The Manager will plan, create, schedule and publish content on these platforms: [Platforms, e.g. Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok].

Each month the Manager will deliver:

- [Number] feed posts per platform
- [Number] stories or short videos per week
- One content calendar for the coming month
- One monthly report covering reach, engagement and follower change

Content beyond these counts, such as extra reels, event coverage or a launch campaign, is quoted separately before work starts.

## 2. Community Management

The Manager will spend up to [Number] hours per week replying to comments and direct messages, during [Business Hours and Time Zone]. Messages about complaints, refunds, legal issues or press go to the Client within one business day. The Manager will not answer those on the Client's behalf. Evening, weekend and holiday cover is not included unless agreed in writing.

## 3. Content Approval and Turnaround

The Manager sends the next month's content calendar by the [Day] of each month. The Client approves it or requests changes within [3] business days. If the Client does not respond in that time, the calendar counts as approved and the Manager may publish it.

Each calendar includes one round of changes. Further rounds, or rewrites after approval, are billed at [$Hourly Rate] per hour. Same-day or reactive posts requested by the Client are published within [24] business hours where possible.

The Client is responsible for the accuracy of product details, prices, offers and claims it supplies.

## 4. Account Access and Credentials

The Client owns all social media accounts, pages, ad accounts and business manager profiles. The Client will grant the Manager access through each platform's role or partner settings, not by sharing personal passwords, where the platform allows it.

The Manager will keep any credentials confidential, use two-factor authentication where offered and not change account ownership or recovery details. Within [5] business days of this agreement ending, the Manager will return any logins it holds and the Client will remove the Manager's access.

## 5. Fees and Payment

The monthly retainer is [$Amount], invoiced on the [Day] of each month in advance. The first month's retainer is due on signing and secures the start date.

Invoices are due within [14] days. Late balances incur a fee of [Percentage]% per month. If an invoice is more than [15] days overdue, the Manager may pause publishing until it is paid.

## 6. Advertising Spend

Paid advertising budgets are not part of the retainer. The Client pays all ad spend directly to each platform using its own payment method. If the Client asks the Manager to pay for ads, the Client must prepay that amount in full first.

Ad management, if included, is limited to [Number] campaigns per month with a monthly budget of up to [$Amount]. The Manager will not exceed an approved budget without written approval.

## 7. No Guaranteed Results

Social platforms control reach through algorithms the Manager does not control. The Manager does not guarantee any number of followers, likes, views, leads or sales. The retainer pays for the services in Section 1, whatever the results.

## 8. Ownership and Usage

Once paid for, the Client owns the content created for its accounts. The Manager may show published posts in its portfolio unless the Client objects in writing. Stock images, music and fonts are used under their own licences. The Client must not reuse platform music in paid ads where the licence does not allow it.

## 9. Term, Cancellation and Termination

This agreement runs for an initial [3] months, then month to month. After the initial term, either party may end it with [30] days' written notice. Fees for the notice period remain payable.

Either party may end the agreement at once if the other materially breaches it and does not fix the breach within [10] days of written notice. The Client pays for all work delivered up to the end date.

## 10. Liability

The Manager is not liable for platform outages, algorithm changes, account suspensions caused by the Client's content or past activity, or lost profits. The Manager's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid in the [3] months before the claim.

## 11. General Terms

The Manager is an independent contractor. Neither party may assign this agreement without the other's written consent. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. This agreement is governed by the laws of [State/Country]. It is the entire agreement between the parties.

## 12. Signatures

By signing below, both parties agree to these terms.
