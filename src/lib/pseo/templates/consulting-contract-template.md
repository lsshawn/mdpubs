This agreement is between [Client Name] ("Client") and [Your Business Name] ("Consultant"), effective [Start Date]. The Consultant will provide advisory services to the Client under the terms below.

## 1. Services

The Consultant will advise the Client on [Subject of Engagement, e.g. pricing strategy, operations review, market entry]. The work includes:

- [Number] discovery interviews with the Client's staff
- Analysis of the data and documents the Client provides
- [Number] working sessions or workshops
- Regular check-in calls of up to [Number] minutes each

Work outside this description, including new questions, extra sites or additional stakeholders, needs a written change order with its own fee.

## 2. Deliverables

The Consultant will deliver:

- [Deliverable 1, e.g. a written findings report]
- [Deliverable 2, e.g. a recommendations slide deck]
- [Deliverable 3, e.g. a 90-day implementation plan]

Deliverables are sent as editable documents. The Client has [5] business days after each delivery to raise factual errors or gaps. The Consultant will correct them once at no charge.

## 3. Timeline

The engagement runs from [Start Date] to [End Date]. Deadlines depend on the Client giving timely access to people, data and systems. Delays caused by the Client move the deadlines by the same amount.

## 4. Fees

Choose one:

- **Hourly:** [$Hourly Rate] per hour, billed in [15]-minute increments, capped at [Number] hours unless the Client approves more in writing.
- **Fixed fee:** [$Amount] for the services and deliverables above, with [Percentage]% due on signing and the balance on delivery of the final report.
- **Retainer:** [$Amount] per month for up to [Number] hours of advisory time. Unused hours do not roll over.

## 5. Payment Terms

The Consultant invoices [monthly / at each milestone]. Invoices are due within [30] days. Late amounts incur [Percentage]% interest per month. The Consultant may stop work while any invoice is more than [15] days overdue.

## 6. Expenses

The Client reimburses reasonable expenses the Consultant incurs for this engagement, including travel, lodging and paid data sources, at cost. Any single expense over [$Amount] needs the Client's written approval before it is incurred. Expenses are invoiced monthly with receipts.

## 7. Advice, Not Outcomes

The Consultant gives professional opinions based on the information available at the time. The Client makes all final decisions and is responsible for how it uses the advice. The Consultant does not guarantee revenue, savings, approvals or any other business result. Fees are not tied to outcomes.

## 8. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other's confidential information private and use it only for this engagement. This includes financial data, customer lists, strategy and pricing. It does not cover information that is public, already known to the receiving party or required by law to be disclosed. This duty lasts [2] years after the agreement ends.

## 9. Ownership

Once paid in full, the Client owns the final deliverables. The Consultant keeps ownership of its pre-existing tools, templates, frameworks and general know-how, and may reuse them. The Client gets a licence to use any of these that appear in the deliverables for its internal business.

## 10. Independent Contractor

The Consultant is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner or agent of the Client. The Consultant sets its own hours and methods, provides its own equipment and pays its own taxes and insurance. The Consultant cannot sign contracts or make commitments for the Client.

## 11. Non-Solicitation

During this agreement and for [12] months after it ends, neither party will directly solicit the other's employees or subcontractors to work for it. General job postings are not solicitation.

## 12. Cancellation and Termination

Either party may end this agreement with [14] days' written notice. The Client pays for all time worked and approved expenses up to the end date. For a fixed fee, the Client pays for completed milestones plus a pro-rated share of work in progress. Either party may end it at once for a material breach not fixed within [10] days of notice.

## 13. Liability

The Consultant's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid under it. Neither party is liable for indirect losses, including lost profits or lost business opportunities.

## 14. General Terms

This agreement is governed by the laws of [State/Country]. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. Neither party may assign it without written consent. It is the entire agreement between the parties.

## 15. Signatures

By signing below, both parties agree to these terms.
