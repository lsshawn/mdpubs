faqs:
  - q: 'How long should UGC usage rights last?'
    a: 'Set a fixed term with a start date, such as 3, 6 or 12 months of paid usage starting from the first ad''s launch. Organic posting on the brand''s own pages can run longer at a lower price. Avoid perpetual paid-ad rights inside the base fee. If the brand wants to keep running a video after the term, it pays an extension fee.'
  - q: 'What is the difference between usage rights and whitelisting?'
    a: 'Usage rights let the brand post your video from its own accounts and ads. Whitelisting, including TikTok Spark Ads and Meta partnership ads, lets the brand run ads that appear under your name or handle. That uses your identity and audience trust, so it is priced and time-limited separately.'
  - q: 'Should I send raw footage to the brand?'
    a: 'Only if they pay for it. Raw clips let the brand cut many more ads from one shoot, so price them as an add-on and apply the same usage term. This template lists raw footage as optional so the brand knows it is not included by default.'

    a: 'Yes, once both parties sign it. Disclosure and advertising rules differ by country, so have a lawyer check it for your jurisdiction.'

This agreement is between [Brand Name] ("Client") and [Your Name or Business Name] ("Creator"), effective [Start Date]. The Creator will produce user-generated content for the Client's product, [Product Name], under the terms below.

## 1. Brief

The Client will send a written brief before filming. It covers the product, key messages, claims the Creator may make, words or topics to avoid, and any references. The Creator films in their own style and setting within the brief. Changes to the brief after filming has started are treated as new work.

## 2. Deliverables

The Creator will deliver:

- [Number] videos, each [15-60] seconds, in [9:16 vertical] format
- [Number] hook variations per video (alternative opening 3-5 seconds)
- Captions or on-screen text if requested in the brief
- Optional: raw footage for [Number] videos, if selected in Section 5

Files are delivered by download link in [MP4, 1080p or higher], without music unless the brief asks for licensed audio.

## 3. Timeline

The Creator will deliver first cuts within [10] business days of receiving the product. The Client gives feedback within [5] business days. Final files are delivered within [3] business days of feedback. If the Client gives no feedback in that time, the videos count as approved.

## 4. Revisions

Each video includes one round of revisions for edits within the brief, such as trimming, reordering clips or changing on-screen text. Reshoots caused by the Creator's error are free. A reshoot because the Client changed the brief, product or messaging costs [$Amount] per video.

## 5. Fees and Payment

- Base fee: [$Amount] per video, including [Number] hooks
- Raw footage add-on: [$Amount] per video
- Paid usage: [$Amount] per [3] months (see Section 6)
- Whitelisting or Spark Ads: [$Amount] per [30] days (see Section 7)
- Exclusivity: [$Amount] (see Section 8)

[50]% of the total is due on signing. The balance is due on delivery of final files, within [14] days of invoice. The Client may not publish any video until it is paid in full.

## 6. Usage Rights

Once paid in full, the Creator grants the Client a non-exclusive licence to use the final videos as follows:

- **Organic:** on the Client's own social accounts, website and email for [12] months
- **Paid ads:** on [Platforms, e.g. Meta, TikTok, YouTube] for [3] months from the first ad launch date, in [Territory]

Usage outside these channels, including TV, out-of-home or retail displays, needs a separate written agreement. The Client may edit the videos into shorter cuts but may not change the Creator's words or imply statements the Creator did not make. Extensions are [$Amount] per additional [3] months. The Creator keeps the copyright and may show the videos in their portfolio after the Client's campaign goes live.

## 7. Whitelisting and Spark Ads

If selected, the Creator will give the Client ad access to their account, through a TikTok Spark Ads code or Meta partnership ads permission, for [30] days. The Client will not post organically from the Creator's account, read private messages or change account settings. The Creator may revoke access at the end of the term or if the Client breaches this agreement.

## 8. Exclusivity

If selected, the Creator will not create sponsored content for [Named Competitors or Category, e.g. other protein powder brands] for [30] days after final delivery. No other exclusivity applies.

## 9. Product

The Client ships the product to the Creator at its own cost. The Creator keeps the product after filming. If the product arrives damaged or late, timelines move by the same number of days.

## 10. Disclosure

Where the Creator posts any content on their own accounts, or where whitelisted ads run under the Creator's name, the content will be clearly labelled as a paid partnership in line with FTC endorsement guidance and each platform's branded content tools. The Client will not ask the Creator to hide the relationship or make claims the Creator has not personally checked.

## 11. Cancellation and Termination

If the Client cancels before filming, the deposit is kept and the Creator keeps the product. If the Client cancels after filming, the full base fee is due. Either party may end this agreement for a material breach not fixed within [7] days of written notice. All usage rights end if the Client fails to pay.

## 12. Liability

The Client is responsible for the product's safety and for the accuracy of claims in the brief. The Creator's total liability is limited to the fees paid under this agreement.

## 13. General Terms

The Creator is an independent contractor. This agreement is governed by the laws of [State/Country]. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. It is the entire agreement between the parties.

## 14. Signatures

By signing below, both parties agree to these terms.
