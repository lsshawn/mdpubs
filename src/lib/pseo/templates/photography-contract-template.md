This agreement is between [Client Name] ("Client") and [Your Business Name] ("Photographer"), and starts on the date both parties sign it.

## 1. Services

The Photographer will provide a [session type, e.g. brand, headshot, family, product] photography session on [Session Date] from [Start Time] to [End Time] at [Location]. The session includes [number] outfit or set changes and [number] people in the frame. Extra time on the day is billed at [$Amount] per hour, agreed before it starts.

## 2. Deliverables and Timeline

The Photographer will deliver at least [number] edited images in high-resolution JPEG through an online gallery within [number, e.g. 3] weeks of the session. The Client will choose their final images from a proof gallery within [number] days of receiving it. If the Client is late choosing, the delivery date moves back by the same number of days.

RAW and unedited files are not included. The Photographer may sell them at [$Amount] at their discretion. The Photographer keeps backup copies for [number] months after delivery and is not required to store them after that.

## 3. Fees and Retainer

The total fee is [$Amount]. A non-refundable retainer of [percentage, e.g. 30%] is due at signing and reserves the date. The retainer pays for turning away other bookings and for planning time. The balance is due [on the session date / before the final gallery is released].

## 4. Payment Terms

Invoices are due within [number] days. Late balances incur a fee of [$Amount or percentage] per month. The final gallery stays locked or watermarked until the Client has paid in full.

## 5. Editing and Revisions

Editing covers colour, exposure, cropping and light skin retouching in the Photographer's style. The Client may request [number] rounds of minor changes to the selected images. Heavy retouching, such as body reshaping, background swaps or removing objects, is billed at [$Amount] per image. The Photographer will not apply filters or edits that change the style beyond recognition.

## 6. Usage Licence

The Photographer owns the copyright in every image. On full payment, the Client receives a [personal / commercial] licence as follows:

- **Personal licence:** print, share and post the images for non-business use. No sale, advertising or product use.
- **Commercial licence:** use the images on the Client's website, social accounts and marketing for [term, e.g. 2 years] in [territory]. Paid advertising, packaging and billboards need a separate licence at [$Amount].

The Client may not sell the images, enter them in contests, or claim to have taken them. Credit to the Photographer is [required / appreciated] when posting online.

## 7. Model Releases and Locations

The Client is responsible for getting signed releases from any models, staff or property owners shown in images used commercially. The Photographer will supply a blank release form on request. The Client must also arrange permits or permission for any location that requires them, and pays any location fees.

## 8. Reshoots

If images are lost or unusable through the Photographer's fault, the Photographer will reshoot at no charge or refund the fee for that portion. If the Client asks for a reshoot because of styling, outfit, makeup or a change of mind, it is billed at [$Amount]. Outdoor sessions cancelled for bad weather may be moved once at no charge.

## 9. Cancellation and Rescheduling

The Client may reschedule once with at least [number] days' notice, and the retainer moves to the new date. If the Client cancels, the retainer is kept. If the Client cancels within [number] days of the session, [percentage]% of the total fee is due. If the Photographer must cancel because of illness or emergency, they will offer a new date or refund all payments, including the retainer.

## 10. Portfolio Use

The Photographer may use the images in their portfolio, website and social media. The Client may opt out by ticking here: [ ] No portfolio use. Opting out does not change the fee.

## 11. Liability

The Photographer's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees the Client has paid. The Photographer is not liable for missed shots caused by the Client's lateness, location restrictions, or people who will not cooperate. Neither party is liable for delays caused by events outside their reasonable control.

## 12. Termination

Either party may end this agreement in writing if the other breaks it and does not fix the breach within [number] days of notice. The Client pays for work done up to that date.

## 13. General Terms

This agreement is governed by the laws of [State/Country]. It is the whole agreement between the parties and replaces any earlier discussion. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. If one part is unenforceable, the rest still applies.
