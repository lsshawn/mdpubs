This agreement is between [Client Name] ("Client") and [Your Business Name] ("Designer"), and starts on [Start Date].

## 1. Services and Deliverables

The Designer will design and build a website for [Client's Business] at [Domain Name].

The scope is:
- Up to [Number] unique page templates, including [e.g. Home, About, Services, Contact].
- Responsive layouts for mobile, tablet and desktop.
- Built on [CMS, e.g. WordPress / Webflow / Shopify].
- [Contact form / booking widget / newsletter signup] integration.
- Basic on-page SEO: page titles, meta descriptions and a sitemap.
- One [Number]-minute recorded training session on editing the site.

Copywriting, photography, logo design, blog content, e-commerce products beyond [Number] items, and ongoing SEO are not included unless added in writing.

## 2. Timeline

| Milestone | Target date |
|---|---|
| Content and logins delivered by Client | [Date] |
| Homepage design for approval | [Date] |
| Inner pages built on staging | [Date] |
| Launch | [Date] |

These dates assume the Client meets its content dates and gives feedback within [Number] business days.

## 3. Fees and Deposit

The total fee is [$Amount]. Payments are due as follows:
- [Percentage]% deposit on signing. Work is booked when it is received.
- [Percentage]% on approval of the homepage design.
- [Percentage]% before launch.

## 4. Payment Terms

Invoices are due within [Number] days. Late payments incur [Percentage]% per month. The Designer will not launch the site, or move it off the staging server, until all launch payments are made.

## 5. Revisions and Changes

The fee includes [Number] rounds of design revisions on the homepage and [Number] round on inner pages. New pages, new features, or a change of CMS after approval are change requests. The Designer will quote each one in writing, and it may move the launch date. Extra work is billed at [$Hourly Rate] per hour.

## 6. Client Content and Delays

The Client will supply all copy, images, videos, logos and product data in final form by [Date]. The Client confirms it has the rights to use everything it supplies. If content is late, the launch date moves by at least the same number of days. If the Client does not respond for [Number] days, the project is paused, the payment for the current milestone becomes due, and restarting depends on the Designer's availability. If a finished site waits on staging for more than [Number] days because of the Client, the final payment is due.

## 7. Hosting, Domain and Third-Party Services

The domain, hosting account, email, and paid plugins, themes or apps are registered in the Client's name and paid by the Client. The Client will give the Designer access to them. The Designer is not responsible for downtime, data loss, security breaches or price changes by the host or other third parties. The Client is responsible for renewing the domain and hosting.

## 8. Browser, Device and Accessibility Scope

The site will be tested on the current and previous major versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge, on desktop, iOS and Android. Older browsers are not supported. The Designer will use readable colour contrast, alt text fields, heading structure and keyboard-usable navigation. A full accessibility audit or certification is not included. The Client is responsible for the accessibility of content it adds after launch.

## 9. Launch and Post-Launch Support

Launch means the site is live on the Client's domain. For [Number] days after launch, the Designer will fix defects in the work it built at no charge. This does not cover new features, content edits, or problems caused by the Client, other developers, or plugin, CMS or host updates. After this window, support is available at [$Hourly Rate] per hour or on a care plan of [$Amount] per month.

## 10. Cancellation

If the Client cancels, the deposit and all milestone payments already due are kept, and work done since the last milestone is billed at [$Hourly Rate] per hour.

## 11. Ownership and Usage Rights

On full payment, the Client owns the site design, its content and the custom code written for it. Open-source code, the CMS, and licensed themes, plugins and fonts stay under their own licences. The Designer keeps its general tools, code snippets and know-how, and may show the site in its portfolio.

## 12. Liability

The Designer's total liability is limited to the fees paid under this agreement. The Designer is not liable for lost sales or data. The Client is responsible for backups after launch, legal pages such as privacy policies, and compliance of its own content.

## 13. Termination

Either party may end this agreement with written notice if the other breaches it and does not fix the breach within [Number] days. The Client pays for work done to date. The Designer hands over the files and access paid for.

## 14. General Terms

The Designer is an independent contractor. This is the whole agreement and can only be changed in writing. It is governed by the laws of [State/Country]. Electronic signatures are valid.
