This agreement is between [Client Name] ("Client") and [Your Business Name] ("DJ"), and starts on the date both parties sign it.

## 1. Event Details

The DJ will perform at [Event Type] on [Event Date] at [Venue Name and Address]. Performance time is from [Start Time] to [End Time], a total of [number] hours. The expected guest count is [number]. The Client's contact on the day is [Name, Phone].

## 2. Services and Equipment

The DJ will provide music, a sound system suited to [number] guests, [number] microphones for speeches, and [basic dance-floor lighting / uplighting / none]. MC announcements, such as grand entrances and cake cutting, are [included / not included]. Extra speakers for a separate ceremony or cocktail area cost [$Amount].

## 3. Arrival, Setup and Teardown

The DJ will arrive [number, e.g. 90] minutes before the start time to set up and sound check. Setup and teardown are not part of performance time. The Client will make sure the venue allows access at that time. Teardown takes about [number] minutes after the end time.

## 4. Power, Space and Load-In

The Client will make sure the venue provides:

- A dedicated standard power outlet within [number] feet of the setup area, not shared with catering.
- A table of at least [size] and a level space of [size] for the setup.
- Cover from rain and direct sun for any outdoor setup.
- Parking and a load-in route, including elevator access, close to the setup area.

If these are missing, the DJ will do their best to perform. They are not liable for sound problems or delays caused by them.

## 5. Planning and Music Requests

The Client will send a planning form, including a must-play list, a do-not-play list and the event timeline, at least [number, e.g. 14] days before the event. The DJ will follow the do-not-play list, including when guests request those songs. The DJ will play guest requests if they fit the event and the Client's lists. The DJ chooses the order and mix of songs to keep the dance floor full. Changes to the timeline on the day are accepted where possible.

## 6. Fees and Deposit

The total fee is [$Amount]. A non-refundable deposit of [percentage, e.g. 50%] is due at signing and holds the date. The DJ turns away other bookings for this date, and the deposit pays for that. The balance is due [number, e.g. 7] days before the event.

## 7. Payment Terms and Overtime

Payments are made by [bank transfer / card / other]. Late payments incur a fee of [$Amount]. If the balance is not paid by the due date, the DJ may cancel the booking and keep the deposit.

Overtime is billed at [$Amount] per hour, in 30-minute blocks. Only the Client or their named contact may approve it. Overtime is paid before it starts, or charged to a card on file. The DJ may decline overtime if the venue curfew or another booking prevents it.

## 8. Venue Rules and Sound Limits

The Client is responsible for knowing the venue's rules, including sound limits, curfews and any noise monitoring. If the venue lowers the volume or stops the music early, this is not a breach by the DJ, and the full fee is still due.

## 9. Conduct and Safety

The DJ may pause or end the performance if guests damage equipment, threaten the DJ, or the setup becomes unsafe because of weather or crowding. The Client pays for any damage guests cause to the DJ's equipment. Changes to the agreed timeline or setup after signing may change the fee.

## 10. Cancellation and Postponement

If the Client cancels, the deposit is kept. If the Client cancels within [number, e.g. 30] days of the event, the full fee is due. If the event is postponed, payments move to a new date once, if the DJ is available. If the DJ cannot perform because of illness or emergency, they will offer a replacement DJ of similar experience, approved by the Client. If the Client declines the replacement, all payments, including the deposit, are refunded.

## 11. Liability

The DJ's total liability is limited to the fees paid. The DJ carries [public liability insurance of [$Amount] / no insurance] and will send proof to the venue on request. Neither party is liable for failures caused by events outside their reasonable control.

## 12. Recordings and Promotion

The DJ may photograph or record their setup for promotion, without filming guests close up. The Client may opt out here: [ ] No promotional use. Mixes are performed live and are not delivered as recordings.

## 13. Termination

Either party may end this agreement in writing if the other breaks it and does not fix the breach within [number] days of notice. The section on cancellation applies to payments.

## 14. General Terms

This agreement is governed by the laws of [State/Country]. It is the whole agreement between the parties. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. If one part is unenforceable, the rest still applies.
