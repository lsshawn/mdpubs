This agreement is between [Client Name(s)] ("Client") and [Your Business Name] ("Photographer") for photography of the Client's wedding, and starts on the date both parties sign it.

## 1. Event and Coverage

The Photographer will photograph the wedding on [Wedding Date] at [Ceremony Venue] and [Reception Venue]. Coverage is [number] continuous hours, from [Start Time] to [End Time]. Coverage starts at the agreed time even if the day runs late. Moments outside the coverage window are not included.

## 2. Timeline and Shot List

The Client will send a final timeline, a family group list and the names of a contact person no later than [number, e.g. 14] days before the wedding. The Photographer will follow it as closely as the day allows. Delays caused by hair and makeup, late guests or a late ceremony may cut into planned portrait time. The Photographer is not responsible for shots missed because of those delays.

## 3. Second Shooter

[A second shooter is included for [number] hours / No second shooter is included.] The Photographer chooses and supervises the second shooter. The second shooter's images are delivered together with the Photographer's, edited to match.

## 4. Deliverables

The Client will receive:

- [number] sneak-peek images within [number] days of the wedding.
- At least [number] edited high-resolution images in an online gallery within [number, e.g. 10] weeks of the wedding.
- [Album, prints or other products, if included.]

Editing covers colour, exposure and light retouching in the Photographer's style. The Client may request [number] round of minor edits to album or print selections. RAW files are not delivered. The Photographer keeps backup copies for [number] months after delivery.

## 5. Fees and Retainer

The total fee is [$Amount]. A non-refundable retainer of [percentage, e.g. 30%] is due at signing. Wedding dates cannot be shared between couples, so the retainer pays for the other bookings the Photographer turns away for this date. The balance is due [number, e.g. 14] days before the wedding.

## 6. Payment and Overtime

Invoices are due by the dates above. Late payments incur a fee of [$Amount]. If the Client asks the Photographer to stay past the end time, overtime is billed at [$Amount] per hour, per photographer, in [30-minute] blocks. Overtime must be approved by the Client or their named contact on the day and is due within [number] days.

## 7. Meals and Breaks

For coverage longer than [number, e.g. 6] hours, the Client will provide a hot meal for each crew member, served when guests eat. The crew will take a break of up to [number] minutes during the meal. They will stay on call for any unplanned moments.

## 8. Venue and Officiant Rules

The Client will tell the Photographer of any venue or officiant restrictions before the wedding, such as flash bans, limits on movement during the ceremony, or no-go areas. The Photographer will follow those rules. Images limited by those rules are not grounds for a refund. The Client will make sure the Photographer has access to the venues during coverage.

## 9. Travel

Travel within [number] miles or kilometres of [Home Base] is included. Beyond that, the Client pays [$Amount] per mile or kilometre. For weddings more than [number] hours away, the Client also pays for accommodation the night before or after. Flights and other travel costs are billed at cost.

## 10. Guests and Other Photographers

Guests with cameras and phones can block the big moments, such as the aisle walk. The Client will ask guests to stay out of the aisle during the ceremony. If the Client books another photographer or videographer, the Photographer is not responsible for shots blocked by them.

## 11. Cancellation and Postponement

A wedding is a single event that cannot be restaged. If the Client cancels, the retainer is kept, and:

- More than [number] days before the wedding: no further payment is due.
- Within [number] days of the wedding: [percentage]% of the total fee is due.

If the Client postpones, payments transfer to a new date once, if the Photographer is available. If they are not, the retainer is kept. If the Photographer cannot attend because of illness or emergency, they will try to send a qualified replacement. If no replacement is possible, all payments, including the retainer, are refunded.

## 12. Image Use and Copyright

The Photographer owns the copyright. The Client receives a personal licence to print, share and post the images. Selling the images or using them in advertising needs written permission. The Photographer may use images in their portfolio and submit them to wedding publications unless the Client opts out here: [ ] No portfolio use.

## 13. Liability

The Photographer's total liability is limited to the fees paid. If images are lost through equipment failure or the Photographer's fault, liability is limited to a refund in proportion to the lost coverage.

## 14. Termination

Either party may end this agreement in writing if the other breaks it and does not fix the breach within [number] days of notice. Payments already made are handled under section 11.

## 15. General Terms

This agreement is governed by the laws of [State/Country]. It is the whole agreement between the parties. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. If one part is unenforceable, the rest still applies.
