  This graphic design contract template is for freelance designers who make logos,
  brand identities, packaging, posters and social graphics. Design jobs go wrong in
  predictable places: a client who wants a fifth round of changes for free, asks for
  the layered working files six months later, or prints a logo set in a font nobody
  licensed. This contract fixes the number of concepts and revision rounds, says
  whether source files are included, puts font and stock licence costs on the client,
  separates print and digital deliverables, and moves copyright to the client only
  when the final invoice is paid.

faqs:
  - q: 'Should a graphic designer hand over source files?'
    a: 'Only if the contract says so. Many designers deliver final exports (PDF, PNG, SVG) by default and charge separately for layered working files, because those let the client edit the design without you. Decide before you quote, write it in the deliverables list, and price it. Saying nothing invites a free request months later.'
  - q: 'Who pays for fonts used in a logo or brand identity?'
    a: 'The client should. A desktop font licence is normally tied to the person or business using it, and a brand will use the font long after the project ends. This template makes the client responsible for buying commercial licences for any paid typefaces and stock images, and has you list them at handover.'
  - q: 'When does the client own the copyright in my design?'
    a: 'Under this template, on final payment. Until then you keep the rights and the client may only use the work for review. Once paid, the client owns the final approved design. You keep the rejected concepts and the right to show the work in your portfolio unless you agree otherwise.'
  - q: 'Is this template legally binding?'
    a: 'Yes, once both parties sign it, as long as it reflects what you actually agreed. Contract rules vary by place, so have a local lawyer check it for your jurisdiction before you rely on it for large projects.'

This agreement is between [Client Name] ("Client") and [Your Business Name] ("Designer"), and starts on [Start Date].

## 1. Services and Deliverables

The Designer will create [Project Description, e.g. a logo and brand identity for Client's bakery].

Deliverables:
- [Number] initial design concepts, presented as a PDF.
- Development of one chosen concept into the final design.
- Final files in these formats: [e.g. SVG, PNG, PDF, EPS].
- [Optional: a one-page brand guide with colours, typefaces and logo spacing].

Anything not listed above, such as extra sizes, new formats, or additional pieces, is out of scope and will be quoted separately.

## 2. Timeline

The Designer will present concepts within [Number] business days of receiving the deposit and a completed brief. Each revision round will be returned within [Number] business days of the Client's written feedback. The Client agrees to send feedback within [Number] business days. If the Client is silent for more than [Number] days, the Designer may move the project to the back of the schedule.

## 3. Fees and Deposit

The total fee is [$Amount]. A non-refundable deposit of [Percentage]% is due on signing, and work starts when it is received. The balance is due on delivery of final files. Extra work is billed at [$Hourly Rate] per hour.

## 4. Payment Terms

Invoices are due within [Number] days. Late invoices incur a fee of [Percentage]% per month. The Designer may hold final files until the balance is paid.

## 5. Concepts and Revision Rounds

The fee includes [Number] rounds of revisions on the chosen concept. A round is one set of consolidated written feedback from the Client. Changes to colour, type, layout and wording count as revisions. Abandoning the chosen concept, or asking for a new style, is a new direction and will be quoted as new work. Further rounds cost [$Amount] each.

## 6. Source Files

[Choose one:] Layered source files ([e.g. .ai, .psd, .indd, .fig]) are included in the fee. / Source files are not included and can be bought for [$Amount]. The Designer will keep project files for [Number] months after delivery. After that, the Designer is not obliged to store them.

## 7. Fonts, Stock and Third-Party Assets

Fonts, stock photos, illustrations and icons from third parties remain under their owners' licences. The Designer will list every paid typeface and stock asset used in the final design. The Client is responsible for buying the commercial licences needed for its use, including web and app font licences if the Client puts the design online. The Designer is not liable for the Client using assets beyond their licence.

## 8. Print and Digital Production

Digital files will be supplied in RGB for screens. Print files will be supplied in [CMYK / Pantone] with bleed to the printer's spec, if the Client supplies that spec. The Client must approve a physical or PDF proof from the printer before a print run. Colour on screen and paper can differ. The Designer is not responsible for printing errors, printer costs, or reprints after the Client approves a proof.

## 9. Copyright and Usage Rights

All rights in the work stay with the Designer until the full fee is paid. Until then, the Client may use drafts only to review them. On final payment, the Designer assigns the Client copyright in the final approved design. Unused concepts, sketches and rejected options remain the Designer's property. The Designer may show the final work in a portfolio and on social media unless the Client asks in writing for it to stay private until [Date].

The Client confirms it has the right to use any logos, photos or text it supplies.

## 10. Cancellation and Kill Fee

Either party may cancel in writing. If the Client cancels:
- Before concepts are presented: the deposit is kept.
- After concepts are presented: [Percentage]% of the total fee is due.
- After final files are delivered: the full fee is due.

The Client receives no rights in any work until the relevant fee is paid.

## 11. Liability

The Designer's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid. The Client is responsible for checking spelling, facts, and that a name or logo does not conflict with an existing trademark. The Designer does not run trademark searches unless this is added to the scope.

## 12. Termination

Either party may end this agreement if the other breaches it and does not fix the breach within [Number] days of written notice. On termination, the Client pays for all work done to date at [$Hourly Rate] per hour, and the Designer delivers the work paid for.

## 13. General Terms

The Designer is an independent contractor, not an employee. This agreement is the whole agreement between the parties and can only be changed in writing. It is governed by the laws of [State/Country]. If any part is found unenforceable, the rest still applies. Electronic signatures are valid.

## 14. Signatures

By signing below, both parties agree to these terms.
