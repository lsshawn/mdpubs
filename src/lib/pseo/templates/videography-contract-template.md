  This videography contract template is for videographers who film brand videos, events,
  interviews and weddings. Video work has two halves, the shoot and the edit, and most fights
  happen in the edit. A client asks for a fifth round of changes. They want a pop song in the
  background that nobody licensed. Or they ask for every hour of raw footage after paying for a
  three-minute film. This agreement sets the shoot days, the cut lengths, the number of revision
  rounds, who pays for music, and what the client may do with the finished video. It also says

covers:
  - 'Shoot and crew: shoot days, hours, crew size and the equipment brought, so extra cameras or drones are priced up front.'
  - 'Deliverables: each cut by length and aspect ratio, such as a 3-minute film and three 30-second vertical clips.'
  - 'Revision rounds: a set number of rounds per cut, with a clear line between a revision and a new edit.'
  - 'Music licensing: the videographer licenses tracks for the agreed channels; commercial songs the client picks are the client''s cost.'
  - 'Raw footage: not included, sold separately, because unedited files show nothing of the finished work.'
  - 'Usage licence: where the client may publish the video, for how long, and whether paid ads are covered.'
  - 'Releases and locations: who gets on-camera releases and filming permits.'
faqs:
  - q: 'How many revisions should a videography contract include?'
    a: 'Two rounds per cut is a common default. This template lets you set the number. It defines a revision as changes to the existing edit, such as trimming, reordering, swapping a shot or fixing a title. A new concept, new footage or a different song counts as a new edit and is billed at your hourly rate.'
  - q: 'Can my client use any song they want in the video?'
    a: 'Only if it is licensed for where the video will run. Popular songs can get videos muted or taken down on social platforms and expose the client to claims. This template has the videographer use licensed library music. If the client insists on a specific commercial track, they get and pay for the licence.'
  - q: 'Should I give my client the raw footage?'
    a: 'You do not have to. Raw footage is hours of unsorted clips, and it takes time to export, upload and store. This template excludes it by default and lets you name a price if the client wants it. Many videographers also add that raw footage is delivered without colour grading or audio sync.'
  - q: 'Is this template legally binding?'
    a: 'It becomes binding once both parties sign it, as long as it meets the basic rules of contract law where you work. Laws differ by state and country, so have a local lawyer check it before you rely on it.'

This agreement is between [Client Name] ("Client") and [Your Business Name] ("Videographer"), and starts on the date both parties sign it.

## 1. Services

The Videographer will plan, film and edit a [type, e.g. brand, event, interview, wedding] video for the Client. Filming takes place on [Shoot Date(s)] at [Location(s)] for up to [number] hours per day. The crew is [number] people. Equipment includes [number] cameras, audio recording and lighting. Drone footage is [included / not included / available at [$Amount]] and depends on local flight rules on the day.

## 2. Pre-Production

The Client will provide a brief, a list of people to interview and any brand assets by [Date]. The Videographer will send a shot list or outline for approval before the shoot. Changes to the outline after approval may change the fee.

## 3. Deliverables

The Videographer will deliver:

- [number] main film of about [length, e.g. 3 minutes] in [aspect ratio, e.g. 16:9].
- [number] short clips of up to [length] in [aspect ratio, e.g. 9:16] for social media.
- Files in [format, e.g. MP4, 1080p or 4K], delivered by download link.

Captions, subtitles and translations are [included / billed at [$Amount]].

## 4. Timeline

The first cut will be delivered within [number, e.g. 3] weeks of the final shoot day. The Videographer will deliver each revised cut within [number] business days of receiving feedback. The Client will give feedback within [number] business days. Delays in feedback move the delivery date back by the same number of days. If the Client gives no feedback within [number] days, the cut is treated as approved.

## 5. Fees and Deposit

The total fee is [$Amount]. A non-refundable deposit of [percentage, e.g. 50%] is due at signing and reserves the shoot dates. The balance is due on delivery of the final cut. For events and weddings, the balance is due [number] days before the event.

## 6. Payment Terms

Invoices are due within [number] days. Late payments incur a fee of [$Amount or percentage] per month. Cuts are delivered with a watermark until the balance is paid. Extra shoot hours are billed at [$Amount] per hour.

## 7. Revisions

Each cut includes [number, e.g. 2] rounds of revisions. A round is one consolidated list of changes from the Client. Revisions cover trimming, reordering, swapping shots, title and text changes, and colour or audio tweaks. Requests for new footage, a new structure or a new song count as a new edit and are billed at [$Amount] per hour.

## 8. Music Licensing

The Videographer will use music from a licensed library, cleared for [channels, e.g. website, social media, paid ads]. If the Client asks for a specific commercial track, the Client gets and pays for the licence before the edit. The Videographer is not liable for takedowns or claims from music the Client supplied or requested.

## 9. Raw Footage and Project Files

Raw footage and editing project files are not included. Raw footage may be bought for [$Amount] and is delivered as-is, without colour grading or synced audio. The Videographer keeps the footage for [number] months after delivery and may delete it after that.

## 10. Releases and Locations

The Client will get signed releases from staff, customers and other people who appear on camera, and permission to film at locations the Client chooses. The Videographer will supply release forms on request. Any permits or location fees are paid by the Client.

## 11. Usage Licence and Copyright

The Videographer owns the copyright in all footage and edits. On full payment, the Client receives a licence to publish the final video on [channels] in [territory] for [term, e.g. unlimited / 2 years]. Paid advertising and broadcast TV need [written approval / an extra fee of [$Amount]]. The Client may not re-edit the video or remove credits without written consent, except to resize it for a platform. The Videographer may show the video in their portfolio unless the Client opts out here: [ ] No portfolio use.

## 12. Cancellation and Rescheduling

If the Client cancels, the deposit is kept. If the Client cancels within [number] days of the shoot, [percentage]% of the total fee is due. Shoot days may be moved once with [number] days' notice. If bad weather stops an outdoor shoot, both parties will agree a new date at no charge. If the Videographer cannot attend, they will send a qualified replacement or refund all payments.

## 13. Liability and Termination

The Videographer's total liability is limited to the fees paid. The Videographer is not liable for footage lost because of venue restrictions, power failures or people who will not cooperate. Either party may end this agreement in writing if the other breaks it and does not fix the breach within [number] days. The Client pays for work done up to that date.

## 14. General Terms

This agreement is governed by the laws of [State/Country]. It is the whole agreement between the parties. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. If one part is unenforceable, the rest still applies.
