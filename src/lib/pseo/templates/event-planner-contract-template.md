This agreement is between [Client Name] ("Client") and [Your Business Name] ("Planner") for the event described below.

## 1. Event and Services

- Event: [Event Type, e.g. wedding reception / company dinner]
- Date: [Event Date]
- Venue: [Venue Name and Address]
- Estimated guests: [Number]

The Planner will provide [Full Planning / Partial Planning / Day-of Coordination], including:
- [Number] planning meetings, in person or by video.
- Vendor recommendations, quotes and contract review.
- A master timeline, floor plan and vendor contact sheet.
- Budget tracking against the Client's event budget.
- A final walkthrough with the venue on [Date].
- On-site coordination on the event day as set out in section 8.

Design services, rentals, décor installation and guest travel are not included unless listed here.

## 2. Timeline

Vendor bookings will be made in this order: venue by [Date], catering by [Date], other vendors by [Date]. The Client will make decisions within [Number] days of the Planner's request. Final guest count and seating plan are due [Number] days before the event.

## 3. Planning Fee and Deposit

The planning fee is [$Amount]. A non-refundable retainer of [Percentage]% is due on signing and reserves the date. The rest is due in instalments of [$Amount] on [Dates], with the final payment due [Number] days before the event.

## 4. Payment Terms

Invoices are due within [Number] days. Late payments incur [Percentage]% per month. The Planner may stop work, including day-of coordination, if any planning fee is unpaid [Number] days before the event.

## 5. Event Budget, Markups and Commissions

The Client's event budget is [$Amount]. It is the Client's money and separate from the planning fee. The Planner will not commit the Client to spending over budget without written approval. The Planner will disclose in writing any markup on items it buys for the Client, and any commission or referral fee it receives from a vendor, before the Client signs with that vendor. [Optional: The Planner charges a [Percentage]% handling fee on items it buys on the Client's behalf.]

## 6. Vendor Contracts

Vendors will contract directly with the Client, in the Client's name, and the Client pays them directly. The Planner may negotiate and review those contracts but is not a party to them. The Planner is not liable for a vendor's failure, lateness, quality or cancellation, and will help find a replacement where possible. If the Client asks the Planner to sign or pay a vendor on its behalf, the Client must pay those costs in advance.

## 7. Guest Count and Scope Changes

If the guest count changes by more than [Percentage]% from the estimate above, the Planner may reprice the planning fee and on-site staffing in writing. Other changes, such as a new venue, an extra event, or a new date, are quoted in writing before work starts. The Client is responsible for vendor charges caused by any change.

## 8. Day-of Coordination

On the event day the Planner and [Number] assistant(s) will be on site for up to [Number] hours, from [Start Time] to [End Time]. Time beyond that is billed at [$Hourly Rate] per hour per person, in 30-minute blocks. The Client will provide a hot meal and a rest break for on-site staff if the event runs over [Number] hours. Rehearsal attendance is [included / billed at $Amount].

## 9. Cancellation and Postponement

If the Client cancels, the retainer is kept and the following share of the planning fee is due:
- More than [Number] days before the event: [Percentage]%.
- [Number] to [Number] days before: [Percentage]%.
- Within [Number] days: 100%.

One postponement to a date within [Number] months is allowed for [$Amount] if the Planner is available. Vendor cancellation terms are set by each vendor's own contract.

## 10. Force Majeure

Neither party is liable for failing to perform because of events outside its control, such as severe weather, natural disaster, fire, venue closure, serious illness, or official restrictions on gatherings. In that case the parties will first try to move the event to a new date. If no date works, the Planner keeps payment for work already done, and any balance is refunded or credited. The Client is encouraged to buy event insurance.

## 11. Liability

The Planner's total liability is limited to the planning fee paid. The Planner is not responsible for guest behaviour, injury, theft, damage to the venue, or alcohol service. The Client is responsible for any damage caused by its guests.

## 12. Photos and Materials

The Planner may use photos of the event setup in its portfolio and on social media, without naming the Client or showing guests' faces unless the Client agrees. Timelines, floor plans and vendor lists are the Planner's work product and are for this event only.

## 13. Termination

Either party may end this agreement with written notice if the other breaches it and does not fix the breach within [Number] days. The Client pays for work done to date and the Planner hands over vendor contacts and planning documents.

## 14. General Terms

The Planner is an independent contractor. This is the whole agreement and can only be changed in writing. It is governed by the laws of [State/Country]. Electronic signatures are valid.
